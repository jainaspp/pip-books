Pip and the Big Rain皮皮和大雨天

Pitter, patter! After the big dig, a big rain comes. By morning the big hole is full of water, and Rabbit's burrow is flooded too. Pip sits in the mud and wants to give up. But Robin says, “Don't worry. Let's think together.” Then Pip watches a raindrop slide down a leaf… and has an idea. A gentle story about what to do when things go wrong: don't give up, think it through with your friends, and a bad day can turn into something new and good.
