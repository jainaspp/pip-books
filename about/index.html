關於皮皮About Pip

誰在寫這些故事 · Who writes them

Pip the Mole stories are written by PP O in Hong Kong. Pip is a little mole. Robin and Rabbit are with him from the first book. Pom the hedgehog arrives with the first snow, and Season 3 follows a steady light to Old Badger's door.

皮皮繪本的故事，由香港的 PP O 創作。皮皮是一隻小鼴鼠。知更鳥和兔子從第一本就在他身邊。第一場雪帶來刺蝟寶寶蓬蓬。第三輯跟著一點不閃的光，走到河谷裡獾婆婆的門前。

怎樣讀 · How to read them

They are bedtime picture books for ages 3–6. Every page has English and Traditional Chinese. The lines are short and calm, and each book holds one small idea: teamwork, sharing, saying sorry, trying again, and others like them. You can start anywhere. The seasons follow the same friends, and each book still reads on its own.

適合 3–6 歲的睡前繪本。每一頁都有英文和繁體中文。句子短、語氣平靜，每本只放一個小道理，例如一起合作、分享、說對不起、再試一次。可以從任何一本開始。故事跟著同一班朋友走過四季，但每本都可以單獨讀。

插圖 · The pictures

The illustrations are AI-generated. PP O writes the stories and the words, and chooses the order of the books. We say this plainly so families know how the pictures were made.

插圖由 AI 生成。故事和文字由 PP O 編寫，書的次序也由 PP O 決定。我們直說，是想讓家長知道圖畫是怎樣做成的。

檔案和年齡 · Files and age

Each book is a 19-page PDF: a cover, 16 story pages, a read-aloud page, and a back cover. You get a screen file and a print-ready 300 dpi file. Gumroad handles checkout and sends the download. The books are for ages 3–6, read aloud or alongside an early reader.

每本是 19 頁 PDF：封面、16 頁故事、一頁可以跟著唸的句子，和封底。內有螢幕版和 300 dpi 印刷版。結帳和下載由 Gumroad 處理。適合 3–6 歲，可以睡前唸，也可以陪剛開始認字的孩子一起看。
