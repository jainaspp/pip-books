It's autumn in the valley. Old Badger walks very slowly, and she needs her little lantern in her window to find her way home after dark. “Me! I promise!” says Pip. “I'll get it there before dark!” Around the bend is a big pile of crunchy gold leaves. “Let's jump in!” Pip's feet itch to jump too… but he hugs the lantern close and walks past, step by step. “I promised Old Badger. After we deliver the lantern, we'll come back and jump!” A gentle story about keeping a promise.
