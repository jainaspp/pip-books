Squeak, squeak! Winter is here, and Pip runs out of his underground room: “The first snow!” The three friends play on the white hill, until Robin spots some tiny footprints. Whose are they? The footprints lead to a low bush by the pond, and under it a little round ball is shivering. It's a baby hedgehog named Pom, who can't find the way home. A gentle story about caring for others: Rabbit holds Pom close, Pip digs a path to his warm underground room, and Robin brings soft moss and sweet red berries.
